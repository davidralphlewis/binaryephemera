[[BookNotes]] 

Author: [[Benjamín Labatut]]

Semi-fictionalised novel about physicists glimpsing unsolvable realities and going mad, about irrationality and uncertainty in the 20th Century. Focuses mostly on John Von Neumann who was involved in The Manhattan Project and invented Game theory.
- Told as a series of fictional talking heads from real people. Unsure where reality ends and fiction begins.
- About how computing takes us to the limits of human knowledge. Von Neumann invented AI at the start of computing.
- Hard limits drive people crazy - Paul Ehrenfest kills his don because of irrationality in quantum mechanics.
- Last section about AlphaGo. Written like a horror novel, an unknown machine beyond our experience.
- Not sure about the last section - implies sentience when it's not.
