This is from [[Getting-Things-Done-David-Allen-2016]]. While it's important to [[gather-everything-in-an-inbox]], it may be that if you leave things in there you quickly become overwhelmed again. Therefore you must go through each item and [[define-the-next-action]] for everything, moving them out of the inbox.

Think of the inbox as a place where you keep things before you decide what to do with them, rather than a place for long term storage.

A good time to clear out the inbox is the [[Weekly-Review]].
