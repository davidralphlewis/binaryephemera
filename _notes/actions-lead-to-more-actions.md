One reason why you should [[Have-a-bias-towards-action]] is that momentum leads to more actions. It's hard to get started sometimes, but [[doing-is-often-better-than-thinking]]. You should [[Just-Start]] and then see what happens, and then build on what you've done. It's often easier to keep going once you've started something. 

In this way, you can get yourself unstuck.
