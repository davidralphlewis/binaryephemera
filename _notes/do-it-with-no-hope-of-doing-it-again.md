title: Do it with no hope of doing it again
layout: note
date: 2026-07-12
permalink:
---
Because [[all-we-have-is-this-present-moment]], when you are trying to build habits, don't think about the future. Don't think about the [[aim-to-do-things-daily-ish]]. Instead, embrace the fact that you may never do a habit again. That's ok, it's still worth doing. 

Instead, accept that [[some-action-is-better-than-no-action]]. Then in another moment, try to do it again.

This is from [[meditations-for-mortals-oliver-burkeman-2024]].
