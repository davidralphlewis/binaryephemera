[[film-notes]]

Creepy, time distorted film about a volunteer on a remote Cornish Island. Each day she checks some flowers, drops a stone down a well.
- Time is out of join. Scenes happen again from a different perspective. Other cast members turn out to be her.
- Feels like the land is reclaiming her, torturing her through her memories. 
- Deserves a re-watch to catch everything.
- Hugely open to interpretation
