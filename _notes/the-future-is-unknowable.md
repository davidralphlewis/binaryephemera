There is no way to know what will happen to you today, or tomorrow, or at any point in the future. You may get hit by a bus, or you may win the lottery or you may continue normally. So why worry about it?

[[Worry-is-an-attempt-to-control-the-future]], but there is no controlling the future. [[all-we-have-is-this-present-moment]].
