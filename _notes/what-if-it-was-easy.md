A [[Questions-for-Self-Reflection]] that Ivr heard from [[Struthless]] and [[meditations-for-mortals-oliver-burkeman-2024]]. If you are struggling to do something  ask what would it look like if it was easy? How would you structure your day around the task to make it easy? 

In this way it prioritises ease and comfort, reducing the need for complicated systems.
