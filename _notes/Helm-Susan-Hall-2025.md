[[BookNotes]] author: [[Susan Hall]]

Delightful novel with linked short stories about the Helm wind in the Pennines, spanning millennia.

- All humans are less powerful than the wind, even our modern age where we seek to control it.
- Elegiac towards the end as climate change and microplastics may stop it forming.
- Gives voice to the wind - playful, omnipresent. Similar to the voice of Dead Papa Toothwort in [[Lanny-Max-Porter]]] (but more fun), a god watching humanity below.
- Each story works on its own, with some being introduced late.
- Moment of transcendence in the flight between the clouds is glorious.
- Glimpse of something bigger and more power than us and what it does to each ages  13thC seek to banish is, Victorian try to understand, iron age want to worship it, modern to fix.
