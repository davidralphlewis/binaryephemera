As writers we tend to come back to the same images and often the same words. Spend five minutes writing all the cliches you have build up. Then write without using them.

You can also keep the list and eliminate when [[Editing-A-Poem]].

A [[Writing-Exercise]] from [[Monday Night Write club]].

This is similar to [[remove-obvious-words]] but more tailored to your idiosyncracies.
