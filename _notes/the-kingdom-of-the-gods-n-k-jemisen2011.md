[[BookNotes]]

Author: [[N-K-Jemisen]]

Conclusion of the inheritance trilogy. (See also [[the-hundred-thousand-kingdoms-n-k-jemisen-2010]] and [[The-Broken-Kingdoms-N-K-Jemisen-2010]])

- Overall less satisfying trilogy than [[The-Broken-Earth-Trilogy]]. 

But the world is inventive, unique and interesting. Shift in perspective in each novel is ambitious, with big changes in each that have long-lasting consequences. The world shifts between each novel.
- Sieh as a godling is more removed from our perspective- harder to adjust to his point of view.
- As a result, there's a lot more exposition at the start of the novel. Usually the way Jemisen writes is exposition after the event, but it is flipped in this novel.
- Second half is much more successful. Climax is intense and interesting.
