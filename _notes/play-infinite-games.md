Some games have definite ends, like solitaire or monopoly. But in life you can play infinite games, like art or writing, where the aim of the game is not to finish but to continue playing. [[Art-is-Play]] after all. This helps you shift your mindset from winning art (whatever that means) to [[Focus-on-Process-not-the-end-goal]].
