[[BookNotes]]

Author: [[Leonora Carrington]]

- Surreal story that jumps between genres and goes very strange by the end.
- About old people being neglected when they have full lives. 
- Could be seen as death / delusion but never hinted at
- Friend becomes ridiculously wealthy due to finding plutonium.
- Short, precise style that is just narrating what happened but all from her POV.
- Ritual and occult myth become real. Story is a power forrce and is equivalent to magix. 
- Utterly charming and delightful.
