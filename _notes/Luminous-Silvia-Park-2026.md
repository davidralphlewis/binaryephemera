[[BookNotes]] author: [[Silvia Park]]

- Future united society of Korea. Robots part of everyday life, but not fully trusted.
- Jun is trans man, half robotic. Robots become the other, feared and unsure.
- Can be seen as a response to [[Blade Runner]]- Cop hunting down robots, but on side, even when they turn out to be murderous.
- Robots are always a reflection of humanity, like Frankenstein.
- Represent the other, a fear of death, a fear of forgetting memories.
- False memories at end is a bit of a swerve.
- 1st half less successful, slow and not much happening
- Melancholy and sad throughout.
- Builds towards a confrontation that doesn't happen.
- To be a mirror is not enough for sentient creatures.
