One interesting point in the introduction to [[The-Odyssey-Homer-Emily-Wilson-2017]] is that Homer may not exist. He may be an amalagmation of different people over many different years. It may be a shared pen name for a collection of artists. It is oral storytelling and there is no way to know for sure.
