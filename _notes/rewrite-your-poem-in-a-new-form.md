[[Writing-Exercise]] from [[Rachel Oates]]. Take a draft of your poem and rewrite it in a new form. How radical can you be? Can you take your long slam poem and condense it to a [[haiku]]? Or a [[Sonnet]]? What changes? What is missed? What is gained?

[[Burning-Haibun]] is a way to do this within form.
