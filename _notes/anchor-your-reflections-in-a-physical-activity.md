One way to focus your [[Journaling]] if you are struggling to start is to link your reflections to a physical activity you do all the time. In Haruki Murukami's case, this is running, but it could be anything like walking or washing the dishes. What is important is you ground your feelings in the specific details of the action, then you can expand out -[[in-the-specific-is-the-universal]] after all.

## Reference

- This idea is from Haruki Murukami, but I came across it [here](https://readsurrounded.substack.com/p/how-to-journal-like-haruki-murakami)
