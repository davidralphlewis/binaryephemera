Choose an instrumental piece of music, preferably one you can loop a couple of times. Set a timer for 10 minutes and play the music. Do some [[Free-Writing]], seeing what images the music produces. Try to write with the music, not about the actual sounds but more about what emotions they bring up.

This [[Writing-Exercise]] is from [[Monday Night Write club]].
