You should [[Have-a-bias-towards-action]], as [[actions-create-meaning]]. Instead of thinking about what you are going to do, try picking one action and doing it. By making a decision and starting, you create momentum. [[actions-lead-to-more-actions]].

Conversely, if you sit around and think a lot, the possibilities seem endless, leading to [[Paralysis-by-analysis]].

See also [[you-have-to-do-the-thing-instead-of-learning-about-it]]
