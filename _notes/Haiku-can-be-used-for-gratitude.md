In [The power of daily haiku](https://youtu.be/O72QqfwYwho?si=IR1ObbINnIH18lKT)  by Zozan Tami, he points that [[Haiku]] can be used as an ongoing [[Gratitude-Journal]]. By taking a moment to notice nature it is a way [[Be-Present]] and to [[slow-down-to-pay-attention]]. The power of this small format means it is well suited for moments of gratitude.

Ive noticed this when I've been keeping a [[Haiku-Journal]].
