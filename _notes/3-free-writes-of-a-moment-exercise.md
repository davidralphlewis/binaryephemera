Pick an important moment. Do three [[Free-Writing]] of 5 minutes each. 

First  focus only on the external world of sense memory. Use [[The-Five-Senses]].

Secondly,  focus on the literal facts of the situation as if watching yourself extrnally.

Finally, focus on the internal world using metaphorical language.

Then find the best phrases from all of them and combine.

This [[Writing-Exercise]] is from [[Monday Night Write club]]
