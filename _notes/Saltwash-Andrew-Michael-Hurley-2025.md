[[BookNotes]] author: [[Andrew Michael Hurley]]

Spoilers below.

- Creepy novella set in a decaying northern seaside town. 
- Two terminal cancer patients meet up, but there's a lottery going on and more people around. Everyone has something nasty they regret. 
- Swerves the supernatural - lottery is for euthanasia. 
- Should these horrible people be let of? What are the limits of mercy? 
- Thematically about the transfer of power between generations. About entropy and Brexit, how the country crumbles. 
- Fantastic writing, stays with you.
