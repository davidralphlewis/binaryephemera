[[BookNotes]] author: [[Yuko Tsushima ]] translator: [[Geraldine Harcourt]]

- Vignettes from a single mother in Japan over a year.
- Frequently sad and melancholic- husband has no interest in raising the child, acts petulant and put out when forced to help.
- Main character frequently drinks, is late to work, hits her daughter. Clear she isn't coping but doesn't acknowledge it.
- Flat becomes it's own character. Beauty in hardship.
- Light is beauty, flat becomes a haven.
