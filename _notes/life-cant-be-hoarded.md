The writing of notes and [[Journaling]] can be seen as an attempt to hoard parts of life, to hold onto different moments so they don't escape. But you can't hoard life. [[You-Will-Die]] and your memories will go with you. You can't save up the best bits for another day or try to hold onto memories. Instead, [[Be-Present]] and focus on now.
