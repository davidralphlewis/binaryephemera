Ideally you should use the simplest possible tools and systems you can to achieve what you want. This is so you avoid the [[System-Maintenance-Trap]]. It's especially useful when starting, as when you [[use-simple-tools-when-starting]] you can [[define-what-is-enough-for-you]].

This is a good reminder not to overcomplicate things. [[Getting-Things-Done-David-Allen-2016]] for example may be useful, but you could also just use a simple to do list.
