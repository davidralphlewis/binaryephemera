This is [[Writing-Exercise]] that involves research. Pick a topic, preferably one you aren't that familiar with. Do some [[Free-Writing]] around what you know, then again about what you don't know.

Then do an initial freewrite. Go away and do some research and do another. Then combine the results.

This is from [[Monday Night Write club]]
