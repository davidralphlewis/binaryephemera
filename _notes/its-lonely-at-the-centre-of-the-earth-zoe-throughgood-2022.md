[[BookNotes]] author [[Zoe Throughgood]]


Autobiographical [[graphic-novel]] about the author going through mental health issues.
Different aspects of her personality are externalised as distinct characters with different styles. Anime girl for moments of extraversion, blank face for masking, large ghibli esque monster for depression.
Formally inventive. Uses the language of comics like Joe Sacco. But obviously to very different purposes.
Not much happens by design, but remains interesting and innovative.
