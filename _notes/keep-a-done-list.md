Instead of focusing of what is to come, what needs to be done in the future, [[Oliver-Burkeman]] recommends keeping a list of things you have done. You shift your attention from the future to a sense of achievement. 

This is the antithesis of [[Getting-Things-Done-David-Allen-2016]].
